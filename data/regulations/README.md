# UNECE Automotive Lighting Regulations Knowledge Base

This directory contains reference regulatory documents for automotive lighting systems, specifically:
- **UNECE Regulation No. 48 (R48)**: Uniform provisions concerning the approval of vehicles with regard to the installation of lighting and light-signalling devices.
- **UNECE Regulation No. 112 (R112)**: Uniform provisions concerning the approval of motor vehicle headlamps emitting an asymmetrical passing-beam or a driving-beam or both and equipped with filament lamps and/or LED modules.

## Official Sources
Official full-text PDF documents can be obtained directly from the United Nations Economic Commission for Europe (UNECE) Transport Division:
1. **UNECE R48**: https://unece.org/transport/vehicle-regulations-wp29/standards/addenda-1958-agreement-regulations-41-60
   - Revision 12 (latest consolidated series 06/07/08)
2. **UNECE R112**: https://unece.org/transport/vehicle-regulations-wp29/standards/addenda-1958-agreement-regulations-101-120
   - Revision 3 / Series 01 amendments
